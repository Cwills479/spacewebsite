Overview

Mars, known as the "Red Planet" due to its iron oxide-rich soil that gives it a reddish hue, is the fourth planet from the Sun and a prime target in the search for extraterrestrial life. Although it’s about half the size of Earth, Mars shares many similarities, such as polar ice caps, seasonal changes, and surface features like valleys, deserts, and the largest volcano in the solar system, Olympus Mons. Its thin atmosphere, composed mostly of carbon dioxide, cannot retain much heat, causing temperatures to vary widely, often dropping well below freezing. Dust storms frequently sweep across the planet, sometimes covering its entire surface. Mars has two small, irregularly shaped moons, Phobos and Deimos, and its surface shows signs of ancient riverbeds and lakebeds, hinting that liquid water once flowed there. These clues fuel ongoing exploration missions, with rovers and orbiters studying the planet’s geology, climate, and potential for past or future life.

Key Facts:

  • Distance from the Sun: 228 million km
  • Diameter: 6779 km
  • Orbital period:687 Earth days
  • Surface temperature: -65°C
