About the Solar System

The Solar System consists of the Sun and the objects that orbit it, including 8 planets, moons, asteroids, comets, and more. The Sun, located at the center, provides the necessary energy for life on Earth and governs the motion of the planets.

Our solar system is part of a larger galaxy, the Milky Way, and is located about 26,000 light years from its center. It has been evolving for over 4.5 billion years, and our understanding of its components continues to grow with advancements in technology and exploration.
