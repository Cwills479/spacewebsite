Overview

Earth is the third planet from the Sun and the only known place in the universe to support life. It has a unique combination of a breathable atmosphere, abundant liquid water, and a protective magnetic field, all of which create a stable environment for diverse ecosystems to thrive. About 71% of Earth’s surface is covered by oceans, while the remaining land hosts mountains, forests, deserts, and plains. Earth’s atmosphere, rich in nitrogen and oxygen, shields the planet from harmful solar radiation and helps regulate temperature. It rotates on its axis every 24 hours, giving us day and night, and takes 365.25 days to orbit the Sun, creating the seasons. Earth’s dynamic geology, driven by plate tectonics, continually shapes its surface, leading to earthquakes, volcanic eruptions, and the formation of mountains. Its rich biodiversity and ability to sustain life make Earth a truly unique planet in the solar system.

Key Facts:

  • Distance from the Sun: 149.60 million km
  • Diameter: 12,756.27 km
  • Orbital period: 24 hours
  • Surface temperature: -25°C to 45°C
